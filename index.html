Aya Mama · in development in Kigali

See the bleed before the body shows it.

Aya Mama is a band and ward system that detects postpartum haemorrhage early, so clinicians can act while there is still time.

45,000 mothers die from bleeding after childbirth every year.1

70% of maternal deaths happen in sub-Saharan Africa.2
~2 hrs from stable to fatal once a bleed takes hold.

The hidden window

Blood pressure looks normal. Until it doesn't.

The body compensates for blood loss, so standard vital signs warn late, and blood loss estimated by eye misses about half of cases. Compensatory reserve falls from the first drop. Aya Mama reads it.

How it works

From admission to discharge, one band follows one mother.

  1. 01

    Fit

    The band goes on her upper arm at admission, on a fresh liner.

  2. 02

    Detect

    It reads compensatory reserve from the shape of every pulse, through labour and on the ward.

  3. 03

    Alert

    Staff watch the colour, not the number. Amber means prepare. Red means go now.

  4. 04

    Refer

    If she needs to move, her data moves with her, so the next team starts informed.

The band

Designed
Reserve88Normal

Worn on the upper arm, where the signal holds steady while she moves. It decides and alarms on its own.

  • Green and infrared optics for darker skin
  • Swappable cells, 24 to 36 hours each
  • Alarms with no network
  • Kinyarwanda and English voice
  • Wipe-clean, fluid-rated
