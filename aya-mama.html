Aya Mama

Two parts. One early warning.

A band that never leaves the mother, and a ward system that lets one clinician watch every bed.

The band

Designed
Reserve88Normal

Worn on the upper arm, where the signal holds steady while she moves. It decides and alarms on its own.

  • Green and infrared optics for darker skin
  • Swappable cells, 24 to 36 hours each
  • Alarms with no network
  • Kinyarwanda and English voice
  • Wipe-clean, fluid-rated

The ward system

Specified

One clinician watches every mother on the ward, ranked by who needs help first.
